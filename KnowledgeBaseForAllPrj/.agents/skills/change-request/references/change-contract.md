# Change contract

An approval-ready change contains: identifier and owner; current state and problem; desired outcome; non-goals; affected components and contracts; alternatives; dependency and data impact; threat and failure analysis; compatibility/migration; rollout and rollback; documentation; acceptance criteria; verification evidence; approvals; unresolved questions.

Risk is `low` when impact is local and reversible, `medium` when multiple components or public behavior change, and `high` when authorization, sensitive data, destructive migration, money, safety, or broad availability is involved. High-risk work needs explicit approval and independent verification.
