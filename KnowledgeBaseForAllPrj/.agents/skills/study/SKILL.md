# Study

1. Frame the decision or learning question, time boundary, source constraints, and required depth.
2. Search the KB first. For unstable, missing, or disputed facts, use primary or authoritative external sources.
3. Decompose genuinely independent questions across agents; give each a non-overlapping question and source rule. Synthesize only after checking their evidence.
4. Separate sourced facts, inference, uncertainty, and recommendations. Prefer direct links and claim-level provenance.
5. Produce an executive summary, evidence table, trade-offs, open questions, and suggested experiments or next steps.
6. If the note should become canonical knowledge, pass it to `$kb-curator`; do not silently promote research notes.

Read [references/research-protocol.md](references/research-protocol.md) when the study has multiple sources or competing claims.
