# Research protocol

Create a claim ledger with: claim, evidence, source type, publication/effective date, confidence, disagreement, and inference flag. Prefer specifications, official documentation, original papers, source code, and first-party datasets over summaries.

For fast-changing topics, state the cutoff date and verify current status. Use at least two independent sources for consequential disputed claims when possible. A citation must support the adjacent claim, not merely discuss the topic.

Synthesis should distinguish established baseline, conditional technique, experimental technique, and rejected option. Recommendations need decision criteria and trade-offs, not a popularity ranking.
